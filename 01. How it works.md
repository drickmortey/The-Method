## What is scripting, really?
<p>
Scripting IS programming, but a special kind at it. 

It's the process of controlling another program via "scripts", this program in our case being the Roblox engine. Think about it, the Roblox engine has 3D graphics, online multiplayer, physics, it's a huge thing. With only Luau and no engine, we're practically in a void. We can't do anything useful other than use the limited stuff that Luau itself comes with. We need the Roblox engine to extend our functionality.

E.g. Luau doesn't really have a way to create a part in the world, we must use what control Luau gets of the engine to do that. This is called **Engine API**, you can view the entire list of what's included right here at https://create.roblox.com/docs/reference/engine. Seriously it's a gimongous list.

> API stands for Application Programming Interface, it's a way that something with more control gives us access to it for a particular purpose.
</p>
